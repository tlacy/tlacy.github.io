My Sports Journey

Competitive Athletics

I competed at the collegiate level on a 4-year Track and Field scholarship at Texas State University, where I specialized in the Triple Jump and set a school record that I'm proud to have held. I also competed in the Long Jump, 4x400 relay, and Javelin Throw.

Lifelong Athlete

Sports have been a constant throughout my life. In Austin, I've participated in competitive leagues for Basketball, Tennis, and Flag Football. More recently, I've taken up Pickleball, which has quickly become a new favorite.

Fitness & Training

Strength training has been a weekly discipline my entire life, helping me stay competitive and maintain the physical foundation that supports both work and play.

Whether watching or playing, sports continue to inspire me with lessons in teamwork, discipline, and the pursuit of excellence.
