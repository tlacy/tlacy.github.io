## What Was Done

### Interview Prep HTML (`novi-labs-interview-prep.html`)

Complete tone audit of the 17-section Novi Labs CTO interview playbook. The core problem: several scripted answers used a "you need X" sales pattern — framing Scott's situation back at him and then offering Tom as the solution. Experienced CEOs recognize this as rehearsed positioning. All instances removed.

**Changes made:**

1. **Section 7 Q&A closing** — Removed "That scope matters at Novi because the CTO here isn't just a head of product engineering — it's owning all of it as the company scales." → Replaced with "I've operated at that scope and I'd expect to again." (Speaks from experience, not from analyzing his situation at him.)

2. **Section 17 scripted Q&A closing paragraph** — Removed a paragraph that enumerated Novi's specific challenges (scaling, CTO transition, AI trust with operators) and framed Tom as the solution. → Replaced with "I've built through exactly this kind of inflection before — scaling, a leadership transition, technical credibility with enterprise customers who don't forgive overpromising. I'm genuinely excited about what Novi is building and I'd love to be part of it."

3. **Section 8 title** — "Reading Scott Sherwood" → "Understanding Scott Sherwood." The word "reading" implies tactical manipulation.

4. **Section 8 framing** — Added: "Background context — not a playbook for appealing to him, but useful for knowing who you're talking to and whether your instincts align."

5. **Section 8 "What loses the room"** — Renamed to "Authentic pitfalls — things that would also be wrong to fake." Removed a bullet about "Missing the exit/investor angle — Scott Jones is working toward a liquidity event" (wrong name + wrong tone).

6. **Section 8 closing highlight** — Replaced "The frame: Translate every technical point into business impact..." with "The alignment question: Do you care about the same things Scott cares about? If the answer is yes — reliability, accountability, building something that holds at scale — that comes through on its own. If it's not a genuine yes, better to know that now."

7. **Section 9 intro** — "These signal strategic thinking, not just execution capability" → "These are things you genuinely need answered before saying yes. Ask what you actually want to know — 3–4 max, let conversation breathe."

8. **Section 10 highlight** — Removed Tom prescribing Novi's strategy in first person ("The right CTO move isn't to add features...") → Replaced with a framing of the NDN flywheel as a question worth having with Scott, not a position to lead with.

9. **Section 10** — Fixed "Scott Jones's M&A background" typo → "Scott Sherwood's background."

**Key philosophical clarification this session:**
The right interview posture is confident mutual evaluation — "I'm interviewing them as well. I don't need a job — I want one." Not detached hedging. Not sales positioning. Genuine interest + genuine assessment.
