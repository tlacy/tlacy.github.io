Engineering Metrics Framework

A practical approach to measuring engineering effectiveness across 30+ teams delivering both enterprise and SMB solutions

Context: Dual Delivery Model

~20
Enterprise Teams
  • Bi-annual releases
  • On-prem & cloud deployment
  • Services-assisted upgrades
  • Longer feedback cycles
~10
SMB Teams
  • Sprint-based or on-demand releases
  • Multi-tenant cloud environment
  • Instant deployment to all customers
  • Rapid feedback loops

Reliability

Are our systems available and performing when customers need them?

  • Uptime by Product Track availability across all offerings; target: 99.9% for enterprise, 99.99% for SMB multi-tenant
  • SLA Attainment Percentage of contractual SLA commitments met; triggers escalation if <95%
  • Telemetry Scorecard Health of observability: metrics coverage, alert quality, dashboard completeness

Velocity

How efficiently are we delivering value?

  • Team Velocity Trends Varies by team context: deployment frequency (SMB), story flow, WI throughput (Enterprise)
  • Release Schedule Integrity Delivering committed scope on time; track % of planned features shipped vs. deferred

Quality

How well does our software work for customers?

  • Change Failure Rate Percentage of deployments causing regressions; target <5% for SMB, <2% for enterprise
  • Customer Defect Trends Incoming rate vs. fix rate by severity (H/M/L); healthy ratio: fix rate ≥ incoming rate
  • Customer Defect Backlog Total outstanding customer-reported issues trended over time; aging analysis for high-severity
  • Maintenance SLOs Time-to-fix targets by severity: High (24h), Medium (1 week), Low (1 month)

Security

How well are we protecting customer data and maintaining compliance?

  • Secure Score Aggregate security posture across identity, data, devices, apps; target >80/100
  • SOC State & Security Backlog SOC 2 compliance status, summary of open security findings by severity

Focus

Where is engineering effort actually going?

  • Work Breakdown Analysis Time allocation across:
    • Maintenance (keeping the lights on)
    • Technical Debt (paying down accumulated design compromises)
    • Innovation/Roadmap (new features and capabilities)
    Target healthy mix: ~70% roadmap, ~20% maintenance, ~10% tech debt

How to Use This Framework

  • Start simple: Don't try to measure everything at once. Pick 2-3 metrics per category.
  • Context matters: SMB teams should focus more on deployment frequency and change failure rate; enterprise teams on release integrity and upgrade adoption.
  • Balance is key: Velocity without quality leads to technical debt. Quality without velocity misses market opportunities.
  • Make it visible: Display metrics in shared spaces (dashboards, team areas, exec reviews). What gets measured gets improved.
  • Iterate the framework: Review quarterly with engineering leaders. Drop metrics that don't drive action, add ones that matter more.

Additional Resources

Want to implement something similar? Consider these complementary practices:

  • DORA Metrics: Deploy Frequency, Lead Time, MTTR, Change Failure Rate
  • SPACE Framework: Satisfaction, Performance, Activity, Communication, Efficiency
  • Team Health Checks: Qualitative signals to balance quantitative metrics
  • Customer Satisfaction Scores: NPS or CSAT tied to engineering quality
