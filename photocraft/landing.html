Techniques

Cloud-powered compositing

HDR

Exposure Fusion (Mertens)

Bracket 3–7 exposures, align with AlignMTB, merge with Mertens for natural HDR. Great for landscapes and architecture.

Focus Stack

Laplacian Focus Stacking

Combine images focused at different distances into one sharp result. Perfect for macro and close-up work.

Best Blend

Sharpness-Weighted Blend

Score all images by sharpness, blend the top performers. Ideal for wildlife and action where you fire a burst.

Panorama

OpenCV Stitcher

Automatically stitch overlapping horizontal frames into wide panoramas. Works with 30%+ image overlap.
