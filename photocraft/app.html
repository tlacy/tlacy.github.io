How it works

Three steps

1

Create an Album

Albums organize your RAW files by shoot. Create one per project — a landscape bracket set, a macro session, a panorama sequence. Albums are private by default; make one public to share a link with clients.

2

Upload RAW Photos

Open an album and upload your NEF, DNG, CR3, ARW, TIFF, or JPEG files directly to the cloud. Files go straight to S3 via presigned URLs — no server in the middle, no per-file size limits.

3

Composite

Go to ✨ Compositing, select your album, choose photos, and pick a technique. Hit Start Compositing — the job runs in a cloud container using rawpy + OpenCV. Check 📊 Projects for your result.

Compositing techniques

HDR

Exposure Fusion (Mertens)

Bracket 3–7 exposures and merge them into one image with natural tone mapping. AlignMTB handles slight movement between shots — no tripod required, though it helps.

📷 3–7 exposures · ±2 stop bracket · landscapes & architecture
Focus Stack

Laplacian Focus Stacking

Combine images at different focus distances into one fully-sharp result. Each frame contributes its sharpest region using Laplacian-based focus detection.

📷 4–20+ photos · step focus through subject · macro & close-up
Best Blend

Sharpness-Weighted Blend

Score all images by sharpness and blend the top performers. Great for burst shooting — the AI picks the best frames automatically.

📷 2–20+ photos · burst or sequential shots · wildlife, sports & portraits
Panorama

OpenCV Stitcher

Automatically stitch overlapping horizontal frames into a wide panorama. OpenCV detects feature points and blends the seams.

📷 2–20+ frames · 30%+ overlap between shots · wide landscapes & cityscapes

🧠 Not sure which technique to use?

On the Compositing page, hit Ask AI before you start. Claude analyzes your photo metadata — exposure values, file names, focus distances, timestamps — and recommends the best technique for your set. It's a free second opinion before the job runs.
