💴 Money & Payments
💳

IC Card (Suica / Pasmo)

Get at Narita or any major station. Tap on/off for trains, buses, taxis. Keep ¥2,000+ loaded at all times.

🏧

Cash — 7-Eleven ATMs

7-Eleven ATMs reliably accept foreign cards. Carry ¥5,000–10,000 daily. Many small spots are cash-only.

🙅

Do NOT Tip

Tipping is considered rude in Japan. Exceptional service is the baseline.

🚇 Getting Around
🚕

Taxis — GO or Uber

Clean, safe, GPS. Show driver the Navigate button. Starts ~¥750.

🟢

Yamanote Line (Green Loop)

Every 2–3 min. Shibuya · Shinjuku · Harajuku · Ueno · Akihabara · Tokyo Station. Your workhorse line.

🚄

Narita Express

Every 30 min from Narita. ~75 min to Shibuya · ¥3,070. Or pre-booked taxi (~90 min, saves money for 3 people + luggage).

🎌 Etiquette
🤐

Quiet on Trains

No phone calls. Speak softly. Eating while walking is frowned upon in most areas.

👟

Remove Shoes

At temples, traditional restaurants, and ryokan. Look for a raised threshold. Leave shoes pointing outward.

🗑

Almost No Public Trash Cans

Carry a small bag for wrappers. Use convenience store bins when you buy something.
